Our Story
1994

More than three decades of connecting our community with technology.

Pro Computer Center was founded in 1994 by Mr Pua Lai Eng, who grew up in a farming family and developed a strong interest in technology from a young age.

Starting from the local community, the business initially focused on providing computer lessons and education, helping people gain their first exposure to computers and modern technology.

Over the years, Pro Computer Center expanded its services to meet the changing needs of the community — from computers, laptops and accessories to accounting software support, CCTV, networking, printers, LED displays and smart classroom technology.

Today, we continue to combine experience, technical knowledge and practical solutions to help individuals, schools, businesses and organisations use technology with confidence.
