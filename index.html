About Us

Three Decades of Technology Experience

Pro Computer Centre was founded in 1994 by Mr Pua Lai Eng. Coming from a farming family, he developed an early interest in technology and began providing computer lessons and technology education to the local community.

Over the years, the business expanded from computer education into computer sales and repair, accounting software support, networking, CCTV, LED display systems and smart classroom solutions.

Today, Pro Computer Centre serves individuals, schools, government organisations, companies and shops with practical technology solutions.

Our Approach

We believe technology should solve problems, not create them.


From choosing the right computer to installing a complete classroom or LED display system, we aim to provide practical solutions with reliable support.
